QuietLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08.

1. Scope and operator

QuietLedger is a private, non-commercial personal finance project operated by one individual for their own N26 and Revolut payment accounts. It is not a public banking service and does not accept other account holders as users. These public pages provide legal information only; they do not provide access to the application.

Operator: the private individual operating QuietLedger solely for their own personal finances, reachable at the contact address below. QuietLedger is a project name, not a company or a separate legal entity.
Privacy contact: lucaabadluca@icloud.com.

2. Current status and information used

Production account access has not yet been established. The existing connector is sandbox-only. Production access, persistent financial-record storage and financial-data sharing through Hermes are planned capabilities, not claims of an already operational service.

Once separately authorized, account information may include account identifiers, account names, bank and currency, balances, transaction amounts and dates, payment status, transaction references and payment descriptions or counterparty details supplied by the bank. Transactions can incidentally contain information about other people. Connection information may include consent/session references, expiry dates and refresh outcomes.

The purpose is personal spending and income analysis, financial tracking and private dashboards. Information is not collected to advertise to others, sell financial profiles or provide services to third parties.

3. Banks and Enable Banking

Bank account information is obtained through Enable Banking Oy after the account holder completes the required authorization. Bank authentication takes place through the bank and Enable Banking authorization flow, not through these public pages. The application is not intended to collect bank login passwords or one-time authentication codes.

Enable Banking and the relevant bank process information under their own applicable notices and terms. Enable Banking provides the connection to the bank; it is not the host of the private QuietLedger dashboard. See https://enablebanking.com/privacy and the notices presented during authorization.

4. Local storage and access

The existing design uses macOS Keychain for signing-key and session storage, with local configuration and connection metadata. A durable local transaction database is not yet implemented. The intended financial-record store and dashboards are local, not in the public GitHub repository.

The connector uses restricted read-only operations and limits returned information; identifiers are masked in selected outputs. These controls do not anonymize financial data. Local software or agents running with sufficient operating-system permissions may be able to access local information. This notice does not promise hardware-isolated keys, universal encryption of all files, or zero risk of unauthorized access.

5. Hermes and external AI processing

Hermes is the agent interface intended to query or explain selected financial information. A locally running connector does not mean all processing stays on the device: information included in an agent prompt or tool result can be sent to its configured external model provider.

Production financial-data access through Hermes is not currently enabled. Before enabling it, the operator must approve the permitted fields, actual provider and account settings, processing locations, retention and training policies, and any chat, logging, memory, backup or messaging destinations. This policy will be updated to identify the enabled processing. Until then, no production financial-data disclosure to external AI providers is authorized by this policy.

If enabled later, only the information needed for the approved task should be supplied. Model output and conversation records may also contain financial information. No blanket promise of EU-only processing, no training, or immediate provider deletion is made.

6. Retention and deletion

There is no implemented automatic retention schedule for a production financial database. Before that storage is enabled, its retention and deletion rules must be defined and this notice updated. Connection records can persist beyond consent expiry until removed; expiry or revocation is not the same as deleting previously retrieved information.

Stopping access requires ending or revoking the relevant authorization where supported. Deleting local records, exports, backups and any permitted agent/chat records is a separate action. Deletion from the local application does not automatically delete records held by banks, Enable Banking, GitHub, email services or any subsequently approved AI provider; their rules and applicable legal obligations govern those copies.

7. Public legal pages and contact messages

The planned GitHub Pages site contains only these legal documents, without application code, financial records, credentials, forms, advertising, custom analytics or embedded AI tools. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the pages under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Emailing the contact address shares the message and sender details with the operator and their email service. Do not send bank credentials, authorization codes or account statements. Contact messages are retained only as needed to answer the request or meet applicable obligations; no automatic deletion interval is promised.

8. Questions and applicable rights

Contact the operator about access, correction, deletion or concerns. Where applicable data-protection law grants rights, these may include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and complaint to a competent supervisory authority. Their applicability depends on the processing and any personal/household exemption. Bank authorization alone is not a blanket legal basis for every later use of information.

This notice will be updated before materially different data handling is enabled.
